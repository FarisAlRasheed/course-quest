# 01. Interview the user

Ask before building. Use `AskUserQuestion` in batches of at most 4 questions, with short options and a recommended default first. Skip anything the folder already answers (syllabus dates, exam format). Never ask what you can read.

## Batch 1: the goal (ask first, right after the inventory)
1. **Deadline and scope.** Which exam or quiz, which date, and exactly which lectures does it cover? (Syllabus often says; confirm.)
2. **Time and state.** How many days or hours are left, and how ready do they feel? (Brand new / half-revised / just need polishing.) This decides the speedrun length.
3. **Language.** Language of the exam (the site's language) and whether explanations may mix in another language.
4. **Devices and sharing.** Phone, laptop or both? Private, or a link to share with classmates? (The artifact is private until the user shares it from the Share menu. Say so.)

## Batch 2: the exam (skip what past papers already show)
5. **Format.** Written by hand, circle-one, fill-in-the-blank, oral, open-book? Marks per question? Allowed aids?
6. **Past papers.** Are there past papers, tutorials, problem sets, practice exams? Where (folder, Drive link)? Which are the most recent? Which semester is "last semester"?
7. **What do they fear?** Topics they find hardest, topics they skipped in class, and topics they already know well (skip or shorten those).
8. **How they learn.** Prefer reading first, quizzes first, or worked examples? Do they want hints before answers?

## Batch 3: ideas and taste (ask after you have analysed the sources)
9. **Their ideas.** "What would make this site feel right for you?" Offer 3 or 4 ideas drawn from the course (visualizers for X, a speedrun, a mock paper, a formula sheet) and let them add their own.
10. **Tone.** Calm and quiet, playful but adult, serious exam-room? University students: never childish.
11. **Anything to avoid.** Colors, fonts, emojis, animation level (reduced motion?).

## Rules
- After each batch, restate what you understood in two lines. Do not interrogate. Three batches maximum.
- If the user answers "you decide", decide, and say what you chose in one line.
- Record the answers in `course-quest/analysis.md` under "Decisions" so later steps do not re-ask.
